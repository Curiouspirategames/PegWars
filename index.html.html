PEG WARS
The last peg bearer wins
Players
Body Areas (pegs move here from fingers · each player gets their own list)
Area Limits
All 10 fingers are always in the game. Body areas are destinations only — pegs cannot be placed directly on them.
How to Play
Quick guide — each turn pick one action
📌PLACE
  • Put a peg on an opponent's free finger
  • Roll 1 → fail  ·  2–5 → 1 peg  ·  6 → 2 pegs (crit!)
🔄MOVE
  • Move one of an opponent's pegs to a body area
  • Roll 1 → peg falls off  ·  2 → fail  ·  3–5 → success  ·  6 → peg moves + their oldest peg gets twisted 90°
  • On success, roll again — 1: they pick where it lands, 2–6: you pick
✂️REMOVE
  • Remove a peg from an opponent
  • Roll 1–2 → fail  ·  3–5 → 1 peg removed  ·  6 → 2 pegs removed (crit!)
🎲 LUCKY PLAYER
⚔️CHALLENGE
  • Both players roll a d20 — highest wins (ties re-roll)
  • Defender loses → 1 peg on them + you get a free Place, Move, or Torture
  • Challenger loses → 2 pegs on you, then 1 finger peg moves to a body area
  • Natural 1 vs 20 = Critical — winner picks an extra peg location on the loser
🔩TORTURE
  • Target an opponent who has body area pegs
  • Roll 1–2 → fail  ·  3–5 → pick 1 peg  ·  6 → pick 2 pegs
  • Then roll for torture type: squeeze / pinch-pull / twist in place
💙LIVES
  • Each player starts with 3 lives
  • Use a life to remove one of your own pegs
  • Lose all lives → eliminated
💥CHAOS
  • Fires after round 5, then every 2 rounds
  • Random event hits everyone — scatter, explode, gain, move, or wildcard
🏆 Goal
Be the last player with lives remaining. Eliminate your opponents by loading them with pegs until they run out of lives.
⏱ GAME TIMER (OPTIONAL)
When time runs out, the player(s) with the most lives win. Ties broken by fewest total pegs.

Battle Log

Place

Always on an opponent's free finger. Only valid opponents (with free fingers) are shown.
1: Fail  |  2-5: 1 peg on a random free finger  |  6: Critical — 2 pegs on random free fingers

Move

Always moves an opponent's peg. Cannot move your own.
1: Peg falls off  |  2: Fail  |  3-5: Success  |  6: Critical — peg moves AND the victim must twist their oldest remaining peg 90° in place and hold it
On success: destination is always a body area. Roll a d6 — 1 means the wearer picks where it lands, 2-6 means the mover picks.

Remove

Always from an opponent's pegs.
1-2: Fail  |  3-5: 1 peg removed  |  6: Critical — 2 pegs removed

Challenge

Both roll d20. Re-rolls on ties.
Defender loses: 1 peg on a free finger. Challenger also gets a free guaranteed Place, Move, or Torture against the defender.
Challenger loses: 2 pegs placed, then 1 finger peg moves to a random body area.
Critical (nat-1 vs nat-20): same penalties PLUS winner picks one extra peg location on loser.

Chaos

Fires after round 5, then every 2 rounds. Roll a d6:
1: Scatter fingers to body  |  2: Most-loaded −1 peg  |  3: Everyone +1 peg  |  4: 💥 Explosion  |  5: Move most→least loaded  |  6: 🔩 Group Torture  |  7: 🎲 Lucky Player (Place+Move+Torture)  |  8: ⏳ Oldest peg tortures 2nd oldest

Torture

Pick an opponent who has at least one peg.
1-2: Fail  |  3-5: Success — pick 1 peg to torture  |  6: Critical — pick 2 pegs to torture
Then roll again to determine the torture type: 1-2: Remove, squeeze skin, replace  |  3-4: Pinch-pull peg off without opening, replace  |  5-6: Twist peg in place as far as possible then release.
Torture is also available as a bonus action when the challenger wins a challenge.

Elimination

A player is eliminated when they run out of lives (lives reach 0 after using the 💙 Use Life button). The host can also manually eliminate a player who gives up — click 🏳 on their card.
