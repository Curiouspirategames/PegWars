PEG WARS
The last peg bearer wins
Players
Body Areas (pegs move here from fingers · each player gets their own list)
Area Limits
All 10 fingers are always in the game. Body areas are destinations only — pegs cannot be placed directly on them.
⏱ Game Timer
When time runs out, the player(s) with the most lives win. Ties are shared victories.
How to Play
Quick guide — each turn pick one action
📌PLACE
  • Put a peg on an opponent's free finger
  • Roll 1 → fail  ·  2–5 → 1 peg  ·  6 → 2 pegs (crit!)
🔄MOVE
  • Move one of an opponent's pegs to a body area
  • Roll 1 → peg falls off  ·  2 → fail  ·  3–5 → success  ·  6 → peg moves + their oldest peg gets twisted 90°
  • On success, roll again — 1: they pick where it lands, 2–6: you pick
✂️REMOVE
  • Remove a specific peg from an opponent — pick the exact finger or body area
  • Guaranteed — no dice roll needed. The peg is removed immediately.
  • Each player gets 2 removes total for the whole game — use them wisely!
  • Remove is unavailable during End Game (last 5 minutes)
🎲 LUCKY PLAYER
⚔️CHALLENGE
  • Both players roll a d20 — highest wins (ties re-roll)
  • Defender loses → 1 peg on them + you get a free Place, Move, or Torture
  • Challenger loses → 1 peg on a finger + 1 peg directly to a random body area
  • Natural 1 vs 20 = Critical — winner picks an extra peg location on the loser
🔩TORTURE
  • Target an opponent who has body area pegs
  • Roll 1 → critical fail (you torture your own peg!)  ·  2 → fail  ·  3–5 → pick 1 peg  ·  6 → critical! pick 2 pegs
  • Then roll for torture type: squeeze / pinch-pull / twist in place
💙LIVES
  • Each player starts with 3 lives
  • Use a life to remove one of your own pegs
  • Lose all lives → eliminated
💥CHAOS
  • Fires after round 5, then every 2 rounds
  • 9 random events: finger frenzy, body blow, peg swap, group torture, oldest peg rotation, double body, finger scatter, peg storm, or pain poll
🪙COIN FLIP
  • After every turn, a coin is flipped
  • Heads → random effect (add peg to body, add to finger, shift a peg, add to random player, or rarely: remove a peg)
  • Tails → nothing happens
🕊MERCY
  • Available when you have >15 body pegs — a pulsing purple button appears
  • Use once per game on your turn: pick 1 peg to remove, then 5 more are removed randomly
  • Each peg must be flicked or pulled off without opening it. If that's not possible, remove normally and immediately pinch the area.
  • Not available during End Game
⏳END GAME
  • When 5 minutes remain, End Game begins
  • Only Move, Place (body areas), and Torture are allowed
  • Remove and Challenge are disabled
  • Place now sends pegs directly to body areas
🏆 Goal
Be the last player with lives remaining. Eliminate your opponents by loading them with pegs until they run out of lives.

Battle Log

Place

Always on an opponent's free finger. Only valid opponents (with free fingers) are shown.
1: Fail  |  2-5: 1 peg on a random free finger  |  6: Critical — 2 pegs on random free fingers
During End Game (last 5 mins): pegs go directly to body areas instead.

Move

Always moves an opponent's peg. Cannot move your own.
1: Peg falls off  |  2: Fail  |  3-5: Success  |  6: Critical — peg moves AND the victim must twist their oldest remaining peg 90° in place and hold it
On success: destination is always a body area. Roll a d6 — 1 means the wearer picks where it lands, 2-6 means the mover picks.

Remove

Guaranteed — no dice roll. Pick the exact peg to remove from an opponent.
Each player has 2 removes total for the whole game. Not available during End Game.

Challenge

Both roll d20. Re-rolls on ties.
Defender loses: 1 peg on a free finger. Challenger also gets a free guaranteed Place, Move, or Torture against the defender.
Challenger loses: 1 peg on a finger + 1 peg directly to a random body area.
Critical (nat-1 vs nat-20): same penalties PLUS winner picks one extra peg location on loser.
Not available during End Game.

Chaos

Fires after round 5, then every 2 rounds. Roll a d9:
1: 🖐 Finger Frenzy  |  2: 💪 Body Blow  |  3: 🔀 Body Swap  |  4: 🔩 Group Torture  |  5: 🔄 Oldest Peg Rotation  |  6: ➕ Double Body  |  7: 💨 Finger Scatter  |  8: 💥 Peg Storm  |  9: 🗣 Pain Poll

Coin Flip

After every turn, a coin is flipped. Tails = nothing. Heads = one random effect:
Add peg to your body · Add peg to your finger · Shift your body peg · Add to a random player's body · (rarely) Remove a peg from a random player.

Mercy

Available to the active player only, once per game, when you have more than 15 body pegs. Not available during End Game.
Pick 1 peg to remove yourself. Then 5 more are removed at random from your body areas.
How to remove: flick or pull each peg off without opening it if possible. If not, open normally then pinch the area for a few seconds.

Torture

Pick an opponent who has at least one body peg.
1: Critical fail — you torture your own body peg!  |  2: Fail  |  3-5: Success — pick 1 peg to torture  |  6: Critical — pick 2 pegs to torture
Then roll again to determine the torture type: 1-2: Remove, squeeze skin, replace  |  3-4: Pinch-pull peg off without opening, replace  |  5-6: Twist peg in place as far as possible then release.
Torture is also available as a bonus action when the challenger wins a challenge.

End Game

When 5 minutes remain on the timer, End Game begins. Only Move, Place (body areas), and Torture are available. Remove and Challenge are disabled.

Elimination

A player is eliminated when they run out of lives (lives reach 0 after using the 💙 Use Life button). The host can also manually eliminate a player who gives up — click 🏳 on their card.
